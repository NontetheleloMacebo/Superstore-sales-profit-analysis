# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an advanced data analysis and business intelligence dashboard developed using Power BI. The analysis explores sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, discount impact and regional trends using the Superstore dataset.

The project builds on previous analysis and focuses on transforming raw business data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis was conducted to investigate key business performance areas, including:

- Overall sales and profitability performance
- Sales and profit trends over time
- The relationship between discounts and profitability
- Product-level sales and profit performance
- Loss-making products and profitability risks
- Customer purchasing performance
- Regional sales and profitability
- Category and customer segment performance

---

## Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report consists of five interactive dashboard pages:

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of key business performance indicators, including:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Sales
- Profit Margin
- Sales and profitability by region, category and segment
- Sales trends
- Top-performing products

### 2. Sales & Profit Trends
Analyses sales and profitability performance over time, including quarterly trends and changes across the analysis period.

### 3. Discount & Profit Analysis
Investigates the relationship between discount levels and profitability and highlights sub-categories and orders that may present profitability risks.

### 4. Product Performance Analysis
Evaluates product-level sales and profitability to identify high-performing products and products generating losses.

### 5. Customer & Regional Analysis
Examines top customers and compares sales and profitability across regions, categories and customer segments.

## Key Insights

The analysis identified several important business insights:

- Technology generated the highest sales and profitability among the main product categories.
- The West region recorded the strongest overall sales and profit performance.
- Sales performance improved over the analysis period, with 2017 recording the highest sales.
- Profitability varies across discount levels, highlighting the importance of monitoring discounting alongside profit performance.
- Certain products generated strong sales but did not necessarily deliver strong profitability.
- Sean Miller was identified as the highest-value customer based on sales performance.
